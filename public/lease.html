TENANT: —, hereinafter referred to as "Tenant." Each Tenant is jointly and severally liable to Landlord for payment of rent and performance of all obligations under this Agreement.

Application Reference Number: —

4. Lease Term

This Agreement commences on — and shall continue on a month-to-month basis.

To terminate this lease, Tenant must deliver written notice to Landlord at least 30 days prior to the desired termination date. Landlord must provide Tenant with at least 30 days written notice of termination unless otherwise required by applicable law.

6. Utilities & Inclusions

The following utilities and amenities are included in the monthly rent at no additional charge:

  • Water & Sewage
  • Gas & Electricity
  • High-Speed WiFi / Internet
  • In-Unit Washer & Dryer
  • Heating & Air Conditioning
  • Microwave Oven
  • Parking (where applicable)

Tenant is responsible for any additional services or subscriptions not listed above.

7. Use of Premises & Tenant Obligations

The Premises shall be occupied only by the named Tenant and Tenant's immediate family members as listed in the approved rental application. Subletting or assignment of this lease is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from Landlord.

Tenant shall not use the Premises or any part thereof for any business, commercial, or professional purposes. The Premises is designated as a private residential dwelling only.

Tenant shall:

  • Keep the Premises clean, sanitary, and free from damage
  • Properly dispose of all waste and garbage
  • Refrain from disturbing neighboring tenants or residents
  • Not make any alterations to the Premises without written consent from Landlord
  • Comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws
  • Not engage in any illegal activities on the Premises

Tenant shall be liable for any damage to the Premises, fixtures, or contents caused by Tenant, Tenant's guests, or invitees, beyond normal wear and tear.

8. Security Deposit

Tenant shall deposit with Landlord a refundable security deposit in the amount of —. The security deposit shall be held as security for Tenant's full performance of all obligations under this Agreement.

The security deposit shall not be applied toward any month's rent, including the final month. Landlord shall return the security deposit, less any lawful deductions, within 21 days of the Tenant vacating the Premises and surrendering the keys, provided that:

  • The Premises is returned in the same condition as received, subject to normal wear and tear
  • All rent and other charges have been paid in full
  • All keys, fobs, and access devices have been returned

Deductions from the security deposit may be made for unpaid rent, cleaning costs, or repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear. An itemized statement of any deductions will be provided in writing.

9. Condition of Premises & Move-In Inspection

Tenant acknowledges that a move-in inspection opportunity will be provided on the commencement date. Tenant has 14 calendar days from the move-in date to inspect the Premises and report any pre-existing defects, damages, or maintenance issues to Landlord in writing.

Failure to report within this 14-day period shall constitute Tenant's acceptance of the Premises in its current condition, subject to normal wear and tear. Landlord agrees to address all valid reported issues promptly within reasonable timeframes.

10. Maintenance & Repairs

Landlord is responsible for maintaining the Premises in a habitable condition and for repairing structural defects and major system failures (plumbing, electrical, heating). Tenant must promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs.

Tenant is responsible for minor maintenance tasks including replacing light bulbs, maintaining cleanliness, and promptly reporting any damage or malfunction. Tenant shall not perform any repairs or alterations without prior written approval from Landlord.

11. Landlord's Right of Entry

Landlord shall provide Tenant with at least 24 hours' written or electronic advance notice before entering the Premises for inspections, maintenance, showings, or other lawful purposes. Entry shall be conducted at reasonable hours between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM unless otherwise agreed.

In the case of a genuine emergency (fire, flood, gas leak, or risk to life or property), Landlord may enter the Premises without prior notice.

12. Pets

Pets are permitted only if explicitly approved in Tenant's application and noted in writing by Landlord. Unauthorized pets are grounds for lease termination. Tenant is responsible for any pet-related damage to the Premises.

13. Default & Remedies

Tenant shall be in default of this Agreement if Tenant: (a) fails to pay rent when due and does not cure within 5 days of written notice; (b) violates any material provision of this Agreement and does not cure within 10 days of written notice; (c) abandons the Premises; or (d) engages in illegal activity on the Premises.

In the event of default, Landlord may pursue all remedies available under applicable law, including eviction proceedings. Tenant shall be responsible for all costs associated with enforcing this Agreement, including reasonable attorney's fees.

14. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois. Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved in the courts of Cook County, Illinois.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements, whether written or oral. This Agreement may only be modified in writing signed by both Parties.

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

16. Equal Housing

GreyHaven Residential LLC is an Equal Opportunity Housing Provider. All persons are considered regardless of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or any other characteristic protected by law.
