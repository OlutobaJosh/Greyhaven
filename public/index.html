Term

Utilities & Inclusions

The following utilities and amenities are included in the monthly rent: Water, Gas, Electricity, WiFi/Internet, In-Unit Washer & Dryer, Heating/Cooling, Microwave Oven, and Parking (where applicable). Tenant is responsible for any services beyond those listed.

Use of Premises

The Premises shall be occupied solely by the named Tenant and Tenant's immediate family members listed in the application, and used only for residential purposes. No commercial activity of any kind is permitted. Tenant shall not engage in any activities that create dangerous, hazardous, or unsanitary conditions or that interfere with the rights of other residents.

Tenant shall be liable for any damage to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear caused by Tenant or Tenant's guests or invitees.

Condition of Premises

Tenant acknowledges that an inspection opportunity will be provided upon move-in. Tenant has 14 days from the move-in date to inspect the Premises and report any pre-existing defects or damages to the property manager in writing. Failure to report within this period constitutes acceptance of the Premises in good condition, subject to normal wear and tear.

Security Deposit

The security deposit shall be held by Landlord as security for Tenant's performance of all obligations under this Agreement. The security deposit may not be applied toward any month's rent. Tenant shall be entitled to a full refund of the security deposit within 21 days of vacating the Premises, provided the Premises is returned in the same condition as received, accounting for normal wear and tear.

Entry by Landlord

Landlord shall provide Tenant with at least 24 hours' advance notice before entering the Premises, except in cases of emergency. Entry shall be conducted at reasonable hours and for legitimate purposes including inspection, maintenance, or showing to prospective tenants.

Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state in which the Premises is located. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
